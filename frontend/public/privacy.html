RigRent Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the RigRent mobile app and website (the "Service") collect, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. RigRent is a marketplace where fleet owners list trucks and trailers for rent, truckers rent them, service companies respond to roadside repair and tow requests, and owners post and fill driver jobs.

1. Who is responsible

The Service is operated by Tajinder Rakkar, 2038 E Jensen Ave, Fresno, CA ("we", "us"). Contact: Taj@rdcFresno.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash) and the role you choose (trucker, fleet owner, or service company).
  • Identity and insurance documents: if you verify, the photo of your driver license or insurance document you take or upload, and the details read from it: for a license, full name, license number, date of birth, expiry date, class and state; for insurance, provider, policy number, insured name, expiry date and coverage. We also store the verification result and any note from an administrator.
  • Listings (fleet owners): vehicle details (title, type, make, year, capacity, mileage, rates, description), DOT and MC numbers, VIN, license plate, insurance provider, policy number and expiry, photos, city/location, and exact pickup address, pickup instructions and access code. The exact pickup address, instructions and access code are shown to a renter only after the owner approves that renter's booking.
  • Bookings and inspections: booking dates, estimated miles, status, pickup and return details, inspection videos, odometer and fuel readings, review ratings and comments, and dispute details you submit.
  • Driver profile (truckers who choose to set one up): years of experience, CDL class, endorsements, equipment experience, availability, bio, home state, phone number, and an optional résumé file. Fleet owners and administrators can see your driver profile, including your phone number, when they view your profile; your phone number is not shown in the browse list.
  • Roadside requests, jobs and bids: request titles, descriptions and locations; bids (price, ETA, notes); driver job posts; and job applications with any note you add.
  • Messages you send to other users inside the app.
  • Service area (service companies): the center point and radius you set.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe (see section 4). We store Stripe identifiers and transaction records (amounts, status, fees), not your full card number.

Information collected from your device

  • Location: with your permission, the app reads your device location to fill in your position when you create a listing, request, or job, to search near you, and to show distance. If you have a driver profile and are marked open to work, the app sends your location to our servers about once a minute while the app is open in the foreground; owners browsing available drivers can see roughly where you were last seen. This stops when you turn "open to work" off or leave the app. For active bookings, the app can register geofences around the pickup and return points on your device and, if you grant "Always" location permission, show you a reminder when you arrive; the geofence monitoring runs on your device and this background location is not sent to our servers.
  • Camera, photos and microphone: only when you choose to take or upload a photo or video (documents, listing photos, inspection videos).
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, a token that identifies your device to Expo's push service so we can send alerts about bookings, bids, messages, and verification.
  • On-device storage: your sign-in token (in the device's secure storage on iOS) and your language preference.

We do not include third-party advertising or analytics software in the app. Our hosting providers may process standard technical connection data (such as IP address) when your device contacts the Service.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the marketplace: show listings, process bookings, payments, reviews, disputes, roadside requests and bids, driver jobs and applications, and deliver messages and notifications.
  • To verify licenses and insurance, including automated reading of document images and administrator review.
  • To process subscriptions and payouts.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, comply with law, and maintain our records.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

Other users

Your name and role, listings, reviews and ratings, bids, and driver profile (as described above) are visible to other users as needed to use the marketplace. Counterparties to a booking, job or bid can message you. If you delete your account, past records involving you remain visible to counterparties under the name "Deleted user" (see section 6).

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users or the public. If we are involved in a merger or sale of the business, information may be transferred as part of it.

5. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account (section 6), the items described there are erased immediately. Records that involve other people are retained in anonymized form. Copies of deleted data may remain in our hosting providers' backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete My Account, then enter your password. (Administrator accounts cannot be self-deleted.) Deletion is permanent.

What is erased: your password, license and insurance details and the images you uploaded, your driver profile and résumé, your push tokens, favorites, service area, uploaded files (including listing photos and inspection videos), verification records, and notifications. Your active listings are removed from the marketplace; your open requests, job posts, bids and applications are cancelled or withdrawn; active subscriptions are cancelled; pending bookings are cancelled. Your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and your email with a placeholder, so you can no longer sign in and the same email can later be used to register again.

What is kept: completed and historical bookings, payment and fee records, reviews, messages and job or bid history that involve other users are kept, but are attributed to "Deleted user".

When deletion is blocked: you cannot delete your account while you have an approved or active booking or an open dispute. Finish or resolve it first.

Stripe: a Stripe account you connected is your own Stripe account. Deleting your RigRent account unlinks it; it does not close it. Manage or close it with Stripe.

If you cannot use the app, email Taj@rdcFresno.com from your account email and we will process the request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can update your profile information in the app, turn off "open to work", and turn off location or notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at Taj@rdcFresno.com and we will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Security

Passwords are stored as hashes, connections to the Service use HTTPS, and the sign-in token is kept in the iOS secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults who rent or operate commercial vehicles and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18; if you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate, which may have different data protection laws than where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the effective date. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email where appropriate.
